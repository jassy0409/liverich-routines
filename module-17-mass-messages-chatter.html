Mass Message Strategy

A mass message is not the sale. It is the door. Your job as a chatter is to be ready the moment a fan walks through it — and to turn every reply into a real conversation before the window closes.

Your job in a mass message cycle

You do not write the mass message — your manager does. Your job is what happens after it sends. Every fan who replies is a warm lead. Every warm lead who gets a real, personalised response within the first hour converts at a dramatically higher rate than one who waits three hours for a generic reply. Speed and personalisation are the two variables you control. Both matter enormously.

The mass message reply window

Why the first hour matters most

The reply window is not infinite — it closes fast.

When a fan replies to a mass message, he is in an active, engaged state. He opened it, read it, and responded — that is three micro-commitments in a row. His emotional temperature is higher than it will be in two hours when he's moved on to something else. Your job is to enter that window before it closes and turn his reply into a real conversation.

0–30 minutes after reply: Highest conversion window. Personalise immediately.

30 min – 2 hours: Still warm. Reply with energy. Reference what he said.

2–6 hours: Cooling. Still worth pursuing, but temper expectations. Use a callback from his profile to re-warm him.

6+ hours: Re-engagement territory — treat it like a cold open, not a hot reply.

How to reply to a mass message reply

The worst thing you can do is reply to a mass message reply with another generic message. That's two broadcasts in a row — he feels like a number. Here's how to make every reply feel personal.

The 3-step mass reply formula

Acknowledge his reply → add one personal detail → keep it moving.

Step 1 — Acknowledge: respond to what he actually said, not just "hey." If he said "🔥" — ask what part got him. If he said "interesting" — ask what he means. His reply contains a hook. Use it.

Step 2 — Personal detail: add one thing from fan notes, or from the conversation itself, that proves this isn't copy-paste. His name, something he told you before, his usual vibe.

Step 3 — Keep it moving: end with a question or a light statement that invites a reply. Keep the loop open.

Fan replying to mass message👀👀
✕ Generic replyhaha right?? 😊 want to see the full thing?
✓ Personal replythose eyes 👀 knew you'd react to this one — you always go for this kind of energy 😏 want me to send the full version over?

One extra sentence that references his pattern. That's the difference between a reply that converts and one that doesn't.

When he replies but doesn't buy

He engaged — he just didn't complete. Don't drop him.

The bump — when and how

Mass message bump rules

Maximum 2 bumps per shift. Minimum 2 hours apart. Never to fans who already bought.

A bump is a follow-up message to fans who received the original mass message but haven't replied. Used correctly, it doubles the reply rate. Used incorrectly — too soon, too often, or to wrong segments — it causes unsubscribes and spam flags.

  • 1Send only to active-last-14-days fans. Bumping cold fans who haven't opened messages in weeks damages deliverability and wastes the send.
  • 2Never bump a fan who already replied — even if he didn't buy. He engaged. Bumping him with the same message is spam.
  • 3Change the angle on the bump. A bump that says the same thing as the original is a copy. A bump that approaches from a different angle — different hook, different detail, different question — is a new opportunity.
  • 4Never unsend and resend to game delivery. This is a known tactic that looks spammy to the platform. Only unsend if it was a mistake.

Shantal vs Karina mass message voice

Shantal — warm, personal, one-to-one

Shantal's mass messages must feel like she wrote them to one person. No "hey everyone" energy. No broadcast language. The tone is sincere, personal, first names when possible, warm without being syrupy. A fan should read it and think she had him in mind specifically.

✕ Wrong tone for Shantalhey guys! i just posted something super hot, you're all going to love it!! 🔥🔥
✓ Right tone for Shantali filmed something last night that i've been thinking about sharing for a while… it felt right tonight. i think you'll understand why when you see it 💛

Karina — lowercase, bratty, pulls him in
Karina's mass messages are lowercase, playful, slightly teasing. They feel like a dare more than an announcement. The fan should feel like she's talking to him specifically — not because it's warm, but because she picked him to show this to.

✕ Wrong tone for KarinaHey! New content just dropped, check it out! 😊
✓ Right tone for Karinaokay i filmed something i probably shouldn't have and i'm only sending it to you 👀 open it before i change my mind

Golden rules of mass message strategy

  • ★Mass replies are the highest priority on the page. Everything else pauses when they come in.
  • ★Make every reply feel personal. One extra sentence referenced to him is the difference between a sale and a skip.
  • ★Maximum 2 bumps per shift. Minimum 2 hours between them. Never to fans who already replied.
  • ★Voice rule is non-negotiable. Shantal = warm and personal. Karina = lowercase and bratty. Never swap.
  • ★The first hour after a mass send is the highest-value hour of the shift. Be ready for it.
