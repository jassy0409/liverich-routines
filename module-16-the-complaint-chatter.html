What this module covers

An angry fan is a fan who cares. Handled correctly, a complaint can turn into your most loyal customer. Handled badly, it becomes a chargeback, a bad review, or a ban. This module teaches you how to de-escalate, protect the account, and turn a complaint into a win.

Key principles

  • 1Fan says 'this content wasn't what I expected, I want a refund.' First response?
    Never promise a refund yourself — that's a manager decision. Acknowledge, empathise, and escalate immediately.
    Example: "i'm so sorry this wasn't what you were expecting 🖤 let me flag this for my manager right away"
  • 2Fan is aggressive and threatening a chargeback. What do you do?
    Chargebacks are a legal/financial issue — never negotiate on your own. Escalate to manager immediately, stay calm, document everything.
    Example: Stay calm → acknowledge → escalate → document. Never negotiate a chargeback solo.
  • 3What should you NEVER do when handling a complaint?
    Promising anything financial without manager approval creates liability. Always escalate before making any promises.
    Example: Never promise money, discounts, or free content in a complaint. Always escalate first.
  • 4Fan calms down after your response. What's your next move?
    A resolved complaint is a chance to build loyalty. Follow up warmly — a fan who felt heard and respected often becomes a long-term supporter.
    Example: Issue resolved → warm follow-up → slow relationship rebuild. Don't pitch immediately.
  • 5What is the most important thing to do during ANY complaint?
    Documentation protects the account. Every complaint interaction should be logged and escalated — no exceptions.
    Example: Document everything. Escalate everything. Never handle a complaint solo.
