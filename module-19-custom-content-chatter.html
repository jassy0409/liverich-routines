Custom Content Requests

A custom request is the highest-value individual transaction on the page. Handled correctly, it creates an Enslavement-level bond. Handled wrong, it creates a dispute, a disappointed fan, and a chargeback. This module covers every step.

You do not quote custom prices or confirm customs alone

Custom content pricing, timelines, and what the creator will or won't do are decisions made by the manager — not the chatter. Your job is to gather the request details, hold the fan warmly, and notify your manager immediately. Never quote a price. Never confirm a delivery timeline. Never agree to anything on the creator's behalf.

What a custom request is — and why it matters

Why customs are the highest-value transaction

He's not buying a product. He's buying something made specifically for him.

A custom request means the fan has moved past browsing — he wants something that only he will have. That level of investment is rare and valuable. Fans who receive a well-handled custom become among the most loyal on the page. They come back. They tip. They upgrade. They tell the model she understands them. Every step of the custom process should reinforce that feeling.

The 5-step custom request process

Step 1 · Receive and clarify the request

Understand exactly what he's asking for before you pass it on.

When a fan requests a custom, your first job is to gather the details your manager needs to make a decision. Ask enough to be clear — but don't interrogate him. Keep it warm and conversational.

Fani'd love a custom video from you
✓ Gather details warmlyi love that idea 😊 tell me a little about what you have in mind — what did you picture?

Capture: what type of content, approximate length, any specific details or scenarios he mentioned, and his budget if he volunteers it.

Step 2 · Hold him while you escalate

Never leave him waiting in silence.

After gathering the details, let him know you're taking it seriously — without making any commitments.

✓ Hold message — no commitmenti love this idea honestly 😊 let me make sure i get the details right for you. give me a moment and i'll come back to you with everything you need to know

Then: pass the full request details to your manager with the fan ID, the request summary, and his budget if shared.

Step 3 · Return with the answer from manager

Deliver the price and timeline as if it's the model herself confirming it.

Once your manager has given you the price and availability, return to the fan and deliver it confidently. No apology. No justification. Just warmth and clarity.

✓ Price delivery — warm, clean, no apologyokay so for what you described — something like [brief description] — it would be $[X] 😊 i'd need about [timeline from manager]. does that work for you?

Price stated once, clean, with the timeline. Ball in his court. No negotiation invited.

Step 4 · Handle the "can you do it cheaper?" response

Frame Control from Module 11. Do not discount without manager approval.

Fanthat's a bit much, can you do it for less?
✓ Frame held — no apology, offer an alternativethe price is based on what goes into making it specifically for you 😊 if that doesn't work, i can look at a shorter version — want me to check what that would be?

If he pushes for a discount, check with manager before offering one. Never reduce the price on your own authority.

Step 5 · After delivery — the follow-up

The message after the custom is as important as the custom itself.

Once the custom has been delivered, wait a reasonable time and follow up with a genuine question. This is one of the highest Rapport-building moments on the page. He spent significant money on something made for him. A sincere follow-up shows he mattered — not just his money.

✓ Follow-up after deliveryhey, just checking in — did you get a chance to watch it? i'm genuinely curious what you thought 😊
Fanhonestly it was even better than i imagined. thank you so much
Model · land the momentthat genuinely means a lot ❤️ i put a lot into it. let me know if you ever want something like this again — i already have an idea for a follow-up if you're interested 😏

The seed for the next custom was planted before he even had time to come down from this one. That's the sales cycle of a custom handled perfectly.

What a chatter must never do in a custom conversation

  • ✕Quote a price without manager approval. You do not know what the creator is willing to make or what it's worth. The manager sets the price.
  • ✕Agree to a timeline without manager confirmation. "I'll have it by Friday" is a promise you can't keep on someone else's behalf. Never commit.
  • ✕Accept a request for content outside the creator's limits. If the request is for something outside what the creator does — at any tier — the Soft No applies immediately. Gather what you can, escalate if needed.
  • ✕Discount without approval. Custom pricing is set by the manager for a reason. Offering a discount without checking is giving away the creator's revenue and your manager's authority.
  • ✕Leave him hanging after delivery. The follow-up is part of the custom process. A fan who gets no response after a significant purchase feels transacted, not valued.

Golden rules of custom content

  • ★Gather details, hold warmly, escalate immediately. In that order, every time.
  • ★Never quote a price or a timeline without manager confirmation.
  • ★Deliver the price once, clean, with no apology. Frame Control applies here.
  • ★The follow-up after delivery is not optional. It builds more Rapport than any opener ever could.
  • ★Seed the next custom in the follow-up. A fan who just had a great experience is the most receptive fan on the page.
