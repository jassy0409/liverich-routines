What this module covers

The best PPV sales don't start with a price — they start with a feeling. The tease is how you make a fan lean forward, get curious, and ask for it before you even send it. Master the tease and your conversion rate will never be the same.

Key principles

  • 1When is the RIGHT time to send a PPV?
    A PPV sent cold almost always gets ignored. Build anticipation first — make him want it before you reveal the price.
    Example: Tease → curiosity → ask → PPV. Never skip the tease.
  • 2Which caption is a better tease?
    A good tease creates desire and FOMO before the price is even mentioned. Option B makes him curious and engaged.
    Example: "okay so i shot something last night and i genuinely can't stop thinking about it 👀"
  • 3A fan says 'what kind of content do you have?' What do you do?
    Find out what he wants FIRST. A personalised pitch based on his preferences converts at 3x a generic one.
    Example: "depends... what are you into? 😏 i want to make sure i send you something you'll actually love"
  • 4How many teases before sending a PPV?
    1–2 warm exchanges is the sweet spot. Too few = cold pitch. Too many = you lose momentum.
    Example: Tease once → get a warm reply → tease again with a hint → send PPV with personalised caption.
  • 5Fan says 'just send it, I'll decide after I see it.' What do you do?
    He's warm — don't lose momentum but don't skip the sell either. A teaser description locks in desire before the price hits.
    Example: "okay okay 😏 so it's [brief teaser] — i think you're going to love it" then send.
