What this module covers

This is the final module. Everything you've learned across modules 1–18 comes together here. The Full Circle is about running a complete shift — from first opener to handoff — using every skill you've built. This is the standard.

Key principles

  • 1You start your shift. Fan Marcus (whale, $600 lifetime, quiet 2 weeks) messages first. What do you do?
    Whale + quiet = rekindle play first. Personal, warm, no pitch. The sale comes after the reconnection.
    Example: "hey Marcus — i was literally thinking about you 🥺 how did that project at work go?"
  • 2Mid-shift a new subscriber messages. He hasn't said anything yet. Best opener?
    New subscriber + no message = Module 8 opener. Curiosity and personality first, always.
    Example: "you've been quiet... i was starting to wonder about you 👀"
  • 3Fan buys a $30 PPV. Your shift ends in 30 mins. What do you do?
    A purchase is the warmest moment — try to build on it. If shift ends, leave a detailed handoff so the momentum continues.
    Example: Purchase → bundle tease or upsell attempt → if shift ends, handoff note with exact next move.
  • 4Fan objects to a price mid-conversation. You try Module 12 (The Objection) and he's still hesitant. What's next?
    When objection handling doesn't close it, go to The Chase. Smaller door or angle change keeps the conversation moving without discounting.
    Example: Objection → reframe (M12) → if still no → smaller door or angle change (M6) → never discount.
  • 5End of shift. You had 3 warm fans, 1 whale check-in, and 1 complaint. What's in your handoff?
    Every active fan gets a handoff entry. The next chatter needs to pick up all 5 conversations without dropping momentum on any of them.
    Example: Every active fan gets a handoff note. No exceptions. No fan left without a next move.
