What this module covers

Most fans start small. The upsell is how you guide them up — from a free sub to VIP, from VIP to SVIP, from a $10 PPV to a $100 bundle. Done right it feels like a natural next step, not a sales pitch.

Key principles

  • 1A free page fan has chatted warmly 3 times and bought a $20 PPV. What's the next move?
    Three warm interactions + one purchase = he's ready to hear about VIP. Plant the seed naturally, don't force it.
    Example: "you know the stuff i save for my VIPs is on a completely different level 👀 just saying"
  • 2How do you introduce a VIP upgrade without sounding pushy?
    Curiosity-based upselling always beats price-first selling. Make him want to know more before you mention cost.
    Example: "there's stuff on my VIP that I genuinely can't post here... you'd probably lose your mind 😏"
  • 3A VIP fan is spending regularly. When do you introduce SVIP?
    SVIP is for fans who've proven they invest. Introduce it too early and it feels presumptuous. Wait for the pattern.
    Example: Wait for consistent spend + genuine connection before mentioning the next tier.
  • 4Fan says 'VIP is too expensive.' What's your response?
    Never discount VIP — it devalues the tier. Acknowledge the concern, reframe the value, and let him come to it on his own terms.
    Example: "totally fair — no pressure at all 🖤 whenever you're ready, i'll be here"
  • 5What is the best upsell sequence?
    The relationship comes first. Every purchase is a step toward the next tier — but only when trust is established.
    Example: Relationship → small win → seed → curiosity → upgrade. Never skip the relationship step.
