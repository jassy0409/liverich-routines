What this module covers

Your top 5% of fans generate 50%+ of your revenue. This module is about identifying them, understanding what drives them, and giving them the level of attention that keeps them spending month after month. Whales don't just buy — they invest in a relationship.

Key principles

  • 1What qualifies a fan as a 'whale'?
    Lifetime spend + consistent engagement is the whale marker. Tier alone doesn't qualify — it's about investment over time.
    Example: Track lifetime spend. $500+ with consistent chat = whale status.
  • 2A whale messages you. What's your FIRST priority?
    Whales don't just want content — they want the relationship. Make him feel special before you even think about a pitch.
    Example: Whales feel the difference between a personal reply and a copy-paste response immediately.
  • 3A whale you haven't heard from in 10 days messages again. What do you do?
    Even whales need warmth before pitches. The relationship is what keeps them — not the content.
    Example: "hey you — i was literally just thinking about you 🥺 how have things been?"
  • 4Which is the biggest mistake chatters make with whales?
    Whales can feel a generic response immediately. They know when they're being treated like a number. Personalisation is everything.
    Example: Every whale deserves a response that could only have been written for him, not copy-pasted to 50 others.
  • 5A whale says he feels like the content has been the same lately. What do you do?
    Involving a whale in the content conversation makes him feel valued and invested. He's not just a buyer — he's a partner.
    Example: "okay i want to know exactly what you want to see — tell me your ideal shoot and i'll make it happen 👀"
