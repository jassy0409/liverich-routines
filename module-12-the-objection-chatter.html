What this module covers

Every 'no' has a reason behind it. This module breaks down the most common objections — too expensive, not interested, already seen it, not now — and gives you the exact plays to turn each one into a yes, or at least keep the conversation alive.

Key principles

  • 1Fan says 'it's too expensive.' What's the WORST response?
    Discounting immediately devalues your content and trains fans to always negotiate. Never lead with a discount.
    Example: Never discount first. Reframe value, or keep the door open — but hold the price.
  • 2Fan says 'not tonight.' What do you do?
    'Not tonight' means not now — not never. Keep the conversation alive and warm so he comes back ready to buy.
    Example: "no worries at all 🖤 when do you think you'll be in the mood? i'll save something for you"
  • 3Fan says 'I've seen everything already.' Best response?
    This objection is often about boredom, not content. Curiosity about something new or exclusive reignites interest.
    Example: "have you seen the one I almost didn't post? 👀 I keep going back and forth on sharing it..."
  • 4Fan says 'I'm broke right now.' What's the play?
    A fan who's temporarily broke is still a fan. Keep him warm and engaged — he'll be back when he has money.
    Example: "aw no stress at all 🖤 come keep me company anyway — when do you think things ease up?"
  • 5Fan says 'not interested' after a PPV send. What do you do?
    Not interested in THIS offer doesn't mean not interested in anything. Find out what he wants and come back with that.
    Example: "totally fine! what are you more into? i want to make sure the next thing i send is actually your thing 😏"
