What this module covers

The biggest revenue in this business doesn't come from one big sale — it comes from fans who spend a little every week for years. This module is about building the kind of fan relationships that last months and years, not just days.

Key principles

  • 1What is the single most important driver of long-term fan retention?
    Fans who feel genuinely known stay. Content can be found anywhere. The relationship is what keeps them on THIS page specifically.
    Example: You can't replicate a relationship. That's the only thing that keeps fans long-term.
  • 2Fan has been subscribing for 6 months. He messages less than before. What do you do?
    Long-term fans who go quiet often just need to feel remembered. A personal check-in referencing your shared history is the highest-value move.
    Example: "hey you — 6 months of knowing each other and you go quiet on me? 🥺 everything okay?"
  • 3What is a 'loyalty moment'?
    Acknowledging a spending or relationship milestone makes fans feel seen and valued. It deepens the bond without costing anything.
    Example: "hey i just realised you've been here for 3 months — that actually means a lot to me 🖤"
  • 4How do you transition a transactional fan into a relationship fan?
    Transactional fans buy when they want something. Relationship fans come back because of how they feel. The shift happens through genuine interest.
    Example: Ask one real question per conversation. Remember the answer. Reference it next time. Repeat.
  • 5Fan says 'I feel like you're just here for the money.' What do you say?
    Denial makes it worse. Demonstrating genuine interest in him shows rather than tells. One specific personal question changes the entire dynamic.
    Example: "honestly? i get why it can feel that way sometimes 🖤 but i genuinely enjoy talking to you — tell me something real, how have you actually been?"
