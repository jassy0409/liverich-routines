What this module covers

The first message sets the tone for the entire conversation. A weak opener gets ignored. A strong one gets a reply within minutes. This module teaches you how to open every type of fan — cold, warm, returning, and VIP — with a line they actually want to respond to.

Key principles

  • 1A fan subscribed 3 days ago but hasn't messaged. What's your opener?
    Curiosity and personality beat generic greetings every time. Option C creates intrigue and invites a reply naturally.
    Example: "you've been quiet... i was starting to wonder about you 👀"
  • 2A returning fan messages after 3 weeks silent. What do you open with?
    Memory is your most powerful re-entry tool. Referencing something personal makes him feel seen and valued immediately.
    Example: "hey you — did that thing with your brother ever get sorted? 👀"
  • 3Which opener is MOST likely to get a reply?
    Questions that invite opinion create instant engagement. The fan feels valued and curious at the same time.
    Example: "okay i need your honest opinion on something 🤔"
  • 4A fan has spent $500+ lifetime but hasn't messaged in 2 weeks. Your opener?
    High-value fans who go quiet need to feel remembered, not sold to. Warm re-entry first, pitch later.
    Example: "been thinking about you — how did that work trip go? 🙈"
  • 5What makes a great opener for a fan who just tipped $10 with no message?
    A tip with no message is an invitation to start a conversation. The money came — now build the relationship.
    Example: "that literally just made my whole morning 🥺 tell me something — what has YOUR day been like so far?"
