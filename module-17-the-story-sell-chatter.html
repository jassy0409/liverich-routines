What this module covers

Stories are your most underused sales tool. A well-crafted story — on the wall or in DMs — can warm up 50 fans at once and fill your inbox for the next 3 hours. This module teaches you how to use personality, storytelling, and curiosity to drive conversations and PPV sales.

Key principles

  • 1What is the main purpose of a wall post or story?
    Every wall post and story should have a purpose — either to open a conversation, tease content, or both. Posting for the sake of posting wastes the opportunity.
    Example: Every post has a job: start a conversation, tease something, or both.
  • 2Which wall post caption is more likely to generate DMs?
    Personality and curiosity pull people into the DMs. Generic captions get scrolled past.
    Example: "something happened today and i can't decide if it's funny or embarrassing 😭 someone come talk to me"
  • 3Fan responds to a story asking 'what happened?' What's your next move?
    A story reply is a warm lead. Build the conversation first — don't pitch immediately. The PPV comes naturally after warmth.
    Example: Story reply → conversation → warmth → natural PPV transition.
  • 4How often should personality posts appear vs content/PPV teases?
    Personality builds the relationship and makes fans feel connected. PPV teases monetize that connection. You need both in the right ratio.
    Example: 2 personality posts : 1 PPV tease — roughly. Adjust based on what's working.
  • 5Fan engages with every story but has never bought anything. What's the play?
    Story engagement is a buying signal — he's interested, he just hasn't been asked in the right way. A personal DM referencing his engagement often converts these fans.
    Example: "hey i noticed you've been keeping up with my stories 👀 i appreciate that — wanted to say hi properly"
