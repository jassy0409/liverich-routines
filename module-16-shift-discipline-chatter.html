Shift Discipline & Time Management

Your shift is 8 hours. What you do in the first 30 minutes, who you prioritise in the middle, and how you hand off at the end determines the entire revenue output of that block. This module makes every hour count.

The PHT-to-PST clock rule

All caption tone and time-of-day references must match the fan's US time, not your PHT clock. Aaliyah on graveyard PHT = US morning fans. Trisha on morning PHT = US late afternoon/evening fans. Princess/Rossana on afternoon PHT = US late night fans. Never reference "good morning" if your fans are in their evening. Write for their clock, always.

The shift structure

A shift without structure is just hours. A structured shift has a beginning, a middle, and a handoff — and each section has a specific job.

First 30 min
Orientation & priority scan

Read the handoff notes from the previous chatter. Scan open conversations. Identify your 3 highest-priority fans for this shift — active buyers, fans who sent a message and weren't replied to, VIPs or whales who are online. Do not open new conversations before you've cleared the queue.

Hour 1–3
Active selling window

This is your peak energy window. Run the IRAE framework on warm fans. Make PPV offers to fans who are already in Attraction or Enslavement. If a mass message went out, work every reply first — these are the hottest leads on the page.

Hour 3–6
Rapport maintenance & re-engagement

Check in with VIPs and long-term fans who haven't messaged yet this shift. Use callbacks. Run any re-engagement messages for fans who went quiet recently. Update fan notes for anyone you spoke to in the first block.

Final 30 min
Handoff prep

Write handoff notes for the next chatter: who had active conversations, who is close to a purchase, who needs a follow-up, any fan situations that need watching. Leave the incoming chatter better positioned than you found it.

Fan prioritisation — who to talk to first

You cannot give every fan the same amount of attention in one shift. Prioritise by revenue potential and relationship momentum, not by who messaged most recently.

The handoff note — what to write

What a good handoff looks like

A handoff note is not a novel. It's a fast brief that gives the incoming chatter the three things they need: who needs attention, what the context is, and what the next move should be.

Example handoff note

🔴 James (fan ID or name): Mid-convo. Was close to buying the $35 PPV. Said "maybe after I eat" — that was 2h ago. Check in warmly, don't pitch again immediately. Let him lead.

🟡 Marcus: Came back after 2 weeks quiet. Picked up fine. Hasn't bought yet this shift but mood was good. Good candidate for a callback opener in first hour.

⚪ New sub (no name yet): Messaged "hey" 30 min ago. Got an opener from me. No reply yet. Leave it — follow up after 6h if still quiet.

Mass message: Went out at 11pm PST, 14 replies so far. All replied to except 3 (IDs listed below). Handle those first.

🎯 The incoming chatter should be able to start working in under 2 minutes because of your note.

Hard rules of shift discipline

  • 1The huddle is not optional. Start-of-shift huddle attendance is mandatory. If you miss it, you are entering your shift blind.
  • 2Fan notes are updated during the shift, not at the end. Memory fades. Log while the conversation is fresh.
  • 3Mass message replies are the highest priority on the page. Everything else pauses until all mass message replies have been addressed.
  • 4No unanswered VIP messages at handoff. A VIP who sent a message and got no reply is a churn risk. Every VIP gets a response before you sign off, even if it's just a warm acknowledgement to buy time.
  • 5The handoff note is part of your job. Leaving without writing it is the same as leaving your replacement without a map. It affects the whole team's revenue.

Golden rules of shift discipline

  • ★Structure your shift. Every hour has a job. Know what yours is before you start it.
  • ★Write for the fan's clock — not yours. PHT to PST is the law.
  • ★Prioritise by intent and value — not by recency.
  • ★Your handoff note is as important as your shift performance. The next chatter builds on what you leave them.
  • ★No VIP unanswered at handoff. Ever.
