What this module covers

Revenue doesn't stop when your shift ends — but it will if you don't hand off properly. A clean shift handoff means the next chatter knows exactly who to follow up with, what was discussed, and where the momentum is. This module teaches you to hand off like a professional.

Key principles

  • 1What MUST be included in a shift handoff note?
    A handoff note without context is useless. The next chatter needs name, conversation summary, status, and the next move.
    Example: Name → what happened → where it is now → what to do next. Every time.
  • 2Fan Marcus is mid-conversation and close to buying a bundle. Your shift ends in 10 mins. What do you do?
    Always try to close a warm fan before shift change. If you can't, the note needs to be detailed enough for the next chatter to pick up exactly where you left off.
    Example: Try to close → if not, note: fan name, what was discussed, where the pitch is, what to say next.
  • 3What is the biggest risk of a poor handoff?
    A warm fan who gets a generic opener after a great conversation feels the disconnect immediately. The sale dies with the momentum.
    Example: Poor handoff = lost momentum = lost sale. Every time.
  • 4Best format for a handoff note on a hot lead?
    Structured notes are scannable and actionable. The next chatter should be able to read it in 10 seconds and know exactly what to do.
    Example: Fan / Status / Discussed / Pitched / Next move — short, clear, actionable.
  • 5You're receiving a handoff. A note says 'Kevin is warm, was discussing fitness content.' What do you open with?
    Use the handoff note to pick up exactly where the last chatter left off. Kevin shouldn't feel the shift change at all.
    Example: "hey Kevin — she mentioned you're into fitness... i may have something that was literally made for you 👀"
